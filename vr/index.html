Mira

Mixed reality with hand tracking. Your real room stays visible but dimmed; a nerve network follows each hand and a water surface on the desk answers your movement.

  1. Sit at a desk, headset on, controllers put down.
  2. Press Enter Mira, then place both hands flat on the desk and hold still for a moment.
  3. Move freely: open and close, spread your fingers, touch your fingertips, move across the desk.
  4. After about two minutes press the Meta button to finish. The log is saved automatically.
